Subject: Partnership request – Vylok hromada (Vylok), Zakarpattia, Ukraine

Dear Mayor, dear colleagues,

We are writing on behalf of Vylok hromada in Berehove raion, Zakarpattia
oblast, Ukraine: 11 settlements, about 14 800 residents, located directly on
the EU border (Vylok–Tiszabecs border crossing with Hungary).

We have learned that in June 2026 you supported a Ukrainian community:
«Шведський сміттєвоз на біометані передали громаді на Вінниччині -
i-vin.info». This is why we are turning to you.

Current situation and environmental risks

Today household waste is not collected at all — neither in our hromada
nor in the neighbouring ones. With no centralised system of waste
collection and transport, the environmental damage is growing fast:

  - illegal dumps: waste is dumped uncontrolled in forest belts, along
    roads and on open land;
  - damage to nature: decomposing waste pollutes soil and groundwater,
    and burning dumpsites release toxic substances into the air;
  - quality of life: this is a direct threat to public health and a
    loss of the tourism and recreation potential of our region.

Our solution: a joint public-private utility company

A single small hromada cannot solve this alone. We see the way out in
establishing a joint public-private utility company that will combine
the resources of our and the neighbouring hromadas with the capacity of
the private sector for collection, transport and further sorting of
waste. This format reduces management costs and delivers a reliable
service to the residents of several hromadas at once.

Why we need support

The project requires substantial start-up investment: specialised
vehicles (refuse trucks, containers) and infrastructure for temporary
storage and sorting. Such initial investment is far beyond the budget
of our small hromada, so we are asking for financial, technical or
expert support.

We are open to partnership in the following forms:
  1. co-financing the purchase of specialised vehicles and equipment
     for waste collection and transport;
  2. grant support for environmental and waste-management
     infrastructure projects;
  3. expert and advisory assistance in setting up and launching the
     public-private partnership.

The water dimension of the problem

Vylok lies on the Tisza, the largest tributary of the Danube. Whatever is
not collected as waste in the upper catchment is washed off the banks by
the spring floods: within days the plastic crosses the border at
Tiszabecs, travels down through Hungary, Serbia and Romania and ends up
in the Danube and the Black Sea. According to the Hungarian PET Kupa
(Plastic Cup) initiative, the Tisza carries roughly 250 tonnes of plastic
a year and the Danube around 1,500 tonnes, and most of what volunteers
recover from the Upper Tisza originates in Zakarpattia, where waste
collection infrastructure is missing.

Building a collection and sorting system in our hromada is therefore not
a local amenity project but a direct reduction of pollution in the shared
Danube basin: less plastic in the river, cleaner drinking water, and
protected fisheries and recreation for every community downstream. It
serves the goals of the EU Water Framework Directive, of the Danube River
Protection Convention (ICPDR), to which Ukraine is a contracting party,
and of the EU Strategy for the Danube Region (EUSDR).

Our priority needs:
  1. refuse collection truck (rear loader) — 1 unit.
  2. waste containers 1.1 m3 — 50 units.
  3. tractor with trailer — 1 unit.

We cover customs clearance, transport on the Ukrainian side, maintenance and
insurance. We will report on the use of the equipment with photos and a press
release, and we are ready to sign a twinning memorandum.

We would be sincerely grateful for the opportunity to discuss possible forms
of cooperation in an online meeting or by correspondence. We believe that
together we can stop this environmental crisis and secure cleaner water and a
safe future along the rivers we share.
