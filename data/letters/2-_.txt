Subject: Partnership request – Vylok hromada (Vylok), Zakarpattia, Ukraine

Dear Mayor, dear colleagues,

We are writing on behalf of Vylok hromada in Berehove raion, Zakarpattia oblast, Ukraine: 11
settlements, about 14 800 residents, located directly on the EU border
(Vylok–Tiszabecs border crossing with Hungary).

We have learned that in June 2026 you supported a Ukrainian community: «Шведський сміттєвоз на біометані передали громаді на Вінниччині - i-vin.info». This is why we are turning to you.

We would be grateful if you could consider our hromada as a recipient of
decommissioned municipal equipment — in particular:
  1. refuse collection truck (rear loader) — 1 unit;
  2. waste containers 1.1 m3 — 50 units;
  3. tractor with trailer — 1 unit.

Current situation: waste collection vehicles built in 1991 and 1994.

We cover customs clearance, transport on the Ukrainian side, maintenance and
insurance. We will report on the use of the equipment with photos and a press
release, and we are ready to sign a twinning memorandum.

Kind regards,
Bilak Serhii Volodymyrovych, Chief Architect of the Community
